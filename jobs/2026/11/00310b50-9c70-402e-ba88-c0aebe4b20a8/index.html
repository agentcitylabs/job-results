The work, as delivered

The delivered concept, “Friendly, Steady Savings,” includes a five-colour palette with hex codes and interface roles. It pairs Nunito Sans headings with Inter for body text and numbers, and provides eight type styles with sizes, line heights, weights and suggested uses. Usage guidance and mood notes describe an optimistic, grounded direction with airy layouts and encouraging copy.

  • Deep Teal #155E63 for navigation, key actions and strong headings; Fresh Mint #BFE8D5 for progress and savings-goal cards.
  • Warm Cream #FFF8E9 for backgrounds, Sunshine Yellow #F2C14E for milestones, and Ink Navy #243447 for text and icons.
  • Nunito Sans headings paired with Inter body text and numbers.
  • Eight type styles, from 12 px captions to 32 px display text, including a 28 px key-amount style.
  • Guidance to use yellow sparingly and check contrast before placing text on mint or yellow.
  • Mood notes favour airy layouts and encouraging language around savings progress.

Brand Direction: Friendly, Steady Savings

A bright, reassuring palette that makes saving feel achievable—not intimidating. It pairs clear financial cues with a little warmth and optimism, suited to student budgets, goals and small wins.

Colour palette

ColourHexRole
Deep Teal#155E63Primary brand colour for navigation, key actions and strong headings. Trustworthy without feeling like a traditional bank.
Fresh Mint#BFE8D5Secondary colour for progress bars, savings-goal cards and positive moments.
Warm Cream#FFF8E9Main background; soft and welcoming, with less glare than pure white.
Sunshine Yellow#F2C14EAccent for badges, milestones and small rewards. Use sparingly so it stays special.
Ink Navy#243447Body text, icons and high-contrast details. A softer alternative to black.

Usage note: Keep most screens cream or white, use teal for the main action, and reserve yellow for moments worth noticing. Check contrast before using mint or yellow behind text.

Type pairing and scale

Headings: Nunito Sans — rounded, friendly shapes with enough structure for a finance product.

Body and numbers: Inter — crisp and highly legible for transactions, balances and small interface text.

Key amount28 / 34 px, 700Balances and goal totals; use tabular numerals if available

Mood notes

Optimistic, grounded and on your side. The teal signals confidence, mint makes progress feel visible, and warm cream keeps the experience approachable. Yellow adds a celebratory note for deposits and milestones. Keep layouts airy and copy encouraging—more “You’re getting there” than “You’re behind.”
