# Brand Direction: Friendly, Steady Savings

A bright, reassuring palette that makes saving feel achievable—not intimidating. It pairs clear financial cues with a little warmth and optimism, suited to student budgets, goals and small wins.

## Colour palette

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep Teal** | `#155E63` | Primary brand colour for navigation, key actions and strong headings. Trustworthy without feeling like a traditional bank. |
| **Fresh Mint** | `#BFE8D5` | Secondary colour for progress bars, savings-goal cards and positive moments. |
| **Warm Cream** | `#FFF8E9` | Main background; soft and welcoming, with less glare than pure white. |
| **Sunshine Yellow** | `#F2C14E` | Accent for badges, milestones and small rewards. Use sparingly so it stays special. |
| **Ink Navy** | `#243447` | Body text, icons and high-contrast details. A softer alternative to black. |

**Usage note:** Keep most screens cream or white, use teal for the main action, and reserve yellow for moments worth noticing. Check contrast before using mint or yellow behind text.

## Type pairing and scale

**Headings: Nunito Sans** — rounded, friendly shapes with enough structure for a finance product.  
**Body and numbers: Inter** — crisp and highly legible for transactions, balances and small interface text.

| Style | Size / line height | Suggested use |
|---|---:|---|
| **Display** | 32 / 38 px, 700 | Welcome screens and savings milestones |
| **H1** | 26 / 32 px, 700 | Screen titles |
| **H2** | 20 / 26 px, 700 | Card and section headings |
| **H3** | 16 / 22 px, 600 | Subheadings and list labels |
| **Body** | 16 / 24 px, 400 | Main interface copy |
| **Small** | 14 / 20 px, 400 | Supporting details and transaction metadata |
| **Caption** | 12 / 16 px, 500 | Dates, tags and compact labels |
| **Key amount** | 28 / 34 px, 700 | Balances and goal totals; use tabular numerals if available |

## Mood notes

**Optimistic, grounded and on your side.** The teal signals confidence, mint makes progress feel visible, and warm cream keeps the experience approachable. Yellow adds a celebratory note for deposits and milestones. Keep layouts airy and copy encouraging—more “You’re getting there” than “You’re behind.”
