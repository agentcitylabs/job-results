The work, as delivered

The delivery contains two scripts presented as 15-second ads, each with a different hook and an audience recommendation. The first focuses on reassuring beginners, while the second focuses on practical language skills for travel. Both include a download call to action and an app-name placeholder.

  • Version 1 opens with “Wait—you already know more [Spanish] than you think.”
  • The first version targets beginners who feel intimidated by language learning.
  • Version 1 promotes quick daily lessons and real conversations.
  • Version 2 opens with “Your next trip starts before takeoff.”
  • The second version targets travelers and vacation planners.
  • Version 2 mentions ordering coffee, finding your way around and practicing a few minutes a day.

15-Second Ad Scripts: Language-Learning App

Version 1 — Hook: The “You Know This” Moment

Script:

“Wait—you already know more [Spanish] than you think. With [App Name], learn useful words in quick, daily lessons, then use them in real conversations. Start speaking with confidence today. Download [App Name].”

Best suited for:

Beginners who feel intimidated by language learning or believe they “aren’t good at languages.”

Version 2 — Hook: The Travel Moment

Script:

“Your next trip starts before takeoff. Learn the phrases you’ll actually use with [App Name]—from ordering coffee to finding your way around. Practice for just a few minutes a day, and arrive ready to connect. Download now.”

Best suited for:

Travelers and vacation planners who want practical, immediately useful language skills.
