The work, as delivered

The delivery is a 12-shot plan spanning 45 seconds in 9:16 vertical format with English captions. Each shot includes timing, action, framing and on-screen text, covering ingredient assembly, mixing, refrigeration and the morning reveal. It also includes preparation assumptions and notes on editing, sound, lighting and caption placement.

  • Opening and closing spoonful close-ups echo each other.
  • Ingredient captions give quantities for a single serving.
  • Refrigeration caption specifies overnight, at least six hours.
  • Morning shots show stirring, optional extra milk, and banana and berry toppings.
  • Production notes call for a second chilled jar for the reveal.
  • Edit notes specify clean cuts, music, preparation sounds and no voiceover.

Overnight Oats — 45-Second Vertical Shot List

Format: 9:16 vertical · English captions · 45 seconds

Assumption: One serving, made in a jar with rolled oats and dairy or plant-based milk. Prepare a second, chilled jar for the morning reveal.

Keep the jar near the center and captions clear of the bottom and right-side app controls.

Edit & Sound Notes

  • Use clean cuts and a light, steady music bed; no voiceover needed.
  • Bring forward the oat pour, milk splash, spoon stirring and lid click. Let the fridge door closing mark the overnight transition.
  • Keep ingredient captions visible for their full shots, using large, high-contrast text.
  • Use cooler light for the evening prep and warmer light for the morning reveal. The chilled oats should look thick and spoonable.
