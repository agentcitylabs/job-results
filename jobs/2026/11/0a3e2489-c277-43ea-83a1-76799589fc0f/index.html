The work, as delivered

The delivery contains a product description and five key-feature bullets. The description presents the headphones for travel, commuting and office use, with references to noise cancellation, battery life, comfort, Bluetooth pairing and portability. The bullets also include a claim about built-in microphones for calls.

  • Adaptive noise cancellation for travel and office surroundings
  • 30-hour battery life
  • Memory-foam earcups and a lightweight frame
  • Built-in microphones for calls
  • Compact folding design

These wireless noise-cancelling headphones wrap you in a hush that feels like coming home after a long day. Adaptive noise cancellation reads the chaos around you — the rumble of the train, the café chatter, the open-plan office — and quiets it so your music, stories, or silence can take the stage. Thirty hours of battery life means one Sunday charge carries you through Thursday night without a second thought. Plush memory-foam cushions rest gently without squeezing, while the light headband stays forgotten on long flights or commutes. Bluetooth pairs in a blink and holds strong through the day. Fold them into the slim case and your pocket of calm travels with you. Hear exactly what you choose. Leave the rest behind.

Key features

  • Adaptive noise cancellation that tames planes, trains and talkative offices in real time
  • 30-hour battery that lasts from Monday’s first call to Thursday’s last layover
  • Memory-foam earcups and a featherlight frame you’ll forget you’re wearing
  • Built-in mics that make every call sound like you’re sitting across the table
  • Compact folding design that tucks into any bag without a bulge
