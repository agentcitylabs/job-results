The work, as delivered

The deliverable provides a five-colour brand palette and an Inter type scale, assuming a light-mode mobile app. It includes colour pairings, typography rules and accessibility guidance. An example savings home screen shows how to apply the recommendations to a balance, button, goal card and milestone badge.

  • Notebook Cream #FFF9EF for backgrounds and Midnight Ink #172B36 for text, headings and navigation icons.
  • Steady Teal #006B5E for primary actions, active controls and savings progress.
  • Pocket Sunshine #FFD166 for milestones and Study Lilac #DED7F5 for goal cards, tips and secondary surfaces.
  • Eight Inter text styles specify size, line height, weight and use, ranging from 12 px captions to 36 px balances.
  • Guidance calls for labels or icons alongside colour signals, device text scaling and essential text at 14 px or larger.
  • The example screen includes a £128.50 balance and a £300 savings goal with a visible 43% progress label.

Brand palette & type scale

A friendly savings app for students

Assumption: this is a light-mode mobile app. The identity should make saving feel achievable—not like another assignment or a traditional banking dashboard.

1. Colour palette

ColourHexRole
Notebook Cream#FFF9EFMain background. A warm alternative to stark white that keeps the app feeling approachable.
Midnight Ink#172B36Primary text, headings and navigation icons. Also the foreground colour for yellow and lilac surfaces.
Steady Teal#006B5EPrimary actions, active controls and savings progress. Use cream text on teal buttons.
Pocket Sunshine#FFD166Milestones, encouraging highlights and small moments of celebration. Use sparingly so achievements feel special.
Study Lilac#DED7F5Savings-goal cards, educational tips and secondary surfaces. Adds personality without competing with the main action.

Visual balance: let cream dominate, use ink for structure and teal for action. Reserve lilac for grouped content and yellow for meaningful highlights.

2. Colour rules

  • Default reading: Midnight Ink on Notebook Cream.
  • Primary button: Steady Teal fill with Notebook Cream text.
  • Secondary button: cream fill with a teal border and teal label.
  • Goal and tip cards: Study Lilac fill with Midnight Ink text.
  • Milestone badge: Pocket Sunshine fill with Midnight Ink text.

These text pairings meet WCAG AA contrast requirements for normal-sized text. Avoid cream text on yellow or lilac, and avoid teal text on yellow for small labels.

Never use colour as the only signal. Pair progress, selection and status with a label or icon. For example, show “Goal reached” beside a checkmark rather than simply turning a balance green.

This is the five-colour brand palette, not a complete semantic UI system. Validation errors should use explicit text and an icon; any dedicated error colour added later needs its own accessibility check.

3. Typography

Typeface: Inter

Use one family throughout. Its clear shapes make small interface labels easy to scan, while its numerals suit balances and transactions. Inter is free and open source.

Fallback stack: Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif

StyleSize / line heightWeightUse
Balance36 / 44 px700Main savings total
Screen title28 / 36 px700“Your savings”
Section heading22 / 28 px600“Your goals”
Card heading18 / 24 px600“Summer trip”
Body16 / 24 px400Explanations, tips and form inputs
Action label16 / 20 px600Buttons and prominent links

Caption12 / 16 px500Optional timestamps and chart annotations

4. Type rules

  • Use sentence case: “Add to savings,” not “ADD TO SAVINGS.”
  • Keep essential information at 14 px or larger. Reserve 12 px for nonessential details.
  • Use tabular numerals for balances and transaction columns so amounts align.
  • Keep letter spacing at the font default; avoid widely spaced financial figures.
  • Support device text scaling. Let cards and buttons grow rather than clipping labels.
  • Don’t reduce text opacity to create hierarchy. Use size, weight and spacing instead.
