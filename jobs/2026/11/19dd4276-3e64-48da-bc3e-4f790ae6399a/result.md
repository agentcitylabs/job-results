# Four-Week Plan: Launch a Local Bakery Newsletter

**Goal:** Send the bakery’s first newsletter by the end of Week 4, with a working signup process and a repeatable routine for future issues.

**Assumptions:** This is a small bakery starting from scratch. The newsletter will go out monthly using a simple email platform. The owner can approve decisions, and one staff member can coordinate the work. No website rebuild or paid advertising is needed.

## Who does what

- **Owner:** Sets the goal, approves offers and content, and authorizes any spending.
- **Newsletter coordinator:** A staff member or freelancer who sets up the platform, writes the email, and manages the launch.
- **Counter team:** Invites customers to subscribe and displays signup materials.
- **Reviewer:** Another staff member who checks links, details, and readability.

One person can cover several roles, but a second pair of eyes should check the email before it goes out.

## Tasks, in order

Times below are estimated **hands-on working time**, not elapsed days.

| Order | When | Task and concrete output | Rough time | Who could do it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Week 1 | **Agree on the purpose.** Choose one primary goal, such as bringing regular customers back for seasonal specials. Set a monthly cadence and a consistent sender name. | 45 min | Owner + coordinator |
| 2 | Week 1 | **Choose an email platform.** Pick a tool with signup forms, mobile-friendly templates, unsubscribe handling, and a plan that fits the budget. | 1 hr | Coordinator; owner approves cost |
| 3 | Week 1 | **Set up the account and sender.** Add the bakery’s name, required mailing address, reply-to inbox, and sender verification. If using a bakery-owned domain, configure the platform’s recommended email authentication. | 1–2 hrs | Coordinator; website/domain helper if needed |
| 4 | Week 1 | **Build the signup form.** Ask for email address only, with first name optional. Explain what subscribers will receive and how often. Add a suitable privacy notice and enable confirmation where appropriate. | 1 hr | Coordinator |
| 5 | Week 1 | **Test signup from start to finish.** Subscribe with a test address, check confirmation and welcome messages, and verify that unsubscribing works. | 30 min | Coordinator + reviewer |
| 6 | Week 2 | **Put signup points in place.** Add the form or signup link to the bakery website and social profiles. Print a counter sign with a tested QR code. | 1–1.5 hrs | Coordinator + website helper |
| 7 | Weeks 2–4 | **Invite customers to join.** Publish a launch invitation on social media and have counter staff mention the newsletter when appropriate. Review signups weekly. | About 1 hr total, plus brief customer conversations | Coordinator + counter team |
| 8 | Week 2 | **Plan the first issue.** Choose one main story, one product or seasonal highlight, and one clear action: visit, preorder, or view the menu. Confirm any offer’s dates, terms, and stock limits. | 45 min | Coordinator + owner |
| 9 | Week 2 | **Gather the facts and photos.** Take 2–3 clear bakery photos. Collect accurate prices, opening hours, links, and product details. | 1 hr | Coordinator + baker/owner |
| 10 | Week 3 | **Create a reusable email template.** Use the bakery’s logo or name, readable text, a simple layout, one main button, and the required footer and unsubscribe link. | 1.5 hrs | Coordinator |
| 11 | Week 3 | **Write and build the first issue.** Draft a subject line, preview text, short introduction, featured item, and call to action. Aim for a friendly email customers can read in about a minute. | 1.5–2 hrs | Coordinator |
| 12 | Week 3 | **Get approval.** Check product names, prices, dates, offer terms, image permissions, and whether the bakery can handle the promoted demand. | 30 min | Owner |
| 13 | Early Week 4 | **Send test emails and fix problems.** Check on phone and desktop, click every link, confirm images and text are readable, and make sure replies reach a monitored inbox. | 45 min | Coordinator + reviewer |
| 14 | Week 4 | **Check the recipient list and schedule.** Send only to people who have agreed to receive marketing emails. Remove test contacts and duplicates. Choose a send time that gives customers time to act. | 30 min | Coordinator |
| 15 | By end of Week 4 | **Send the newsletter.** Confirm delivery has started, watch for errors, and respond to customer questions. | 30 min | Coordinator |
| 16 | 2–3 days after sending | **Review and set up the next issue.** Record subscriber count, clicks, unsubscribes, and any attributable orders or offer redemptions. Note one improvement and book the next send date. | 45 min | Coordinator + owner |

**Estimated total:** About **16–18 staff hours**, plus brief customer invitations during normal service. If the first send is late in Week 4, the review falls in early Week 5.

## Weekly checkpoints

- **End of Week 1:** A customer can successfully sign up and unsubscribe.
- **End of Week 2:** Signup links are public, and the first issue’s topic, photos, and facts are ready.
- **End of Week 3:** The email is built and approved.
- **End of Week 4:** The first issue has been tested and sent; the next issue has an owner.

## Keep the launch on track

**Critical sequence:** Platform setup → tested signup → customer invitations → approved email → final test → send.

- **Start collecting signups early.** Don’t wait until the email design is finished.
- **Don’t import the customer order list automatically.** An order email address is not necessarily permission to send marketing. Follow applicable local email and privacy rules.
- **Keep the first issue small.** One good photo, one useful update, and one clear action are enough.
- **If time slips, cut extras—not testing.** Skip elaborate graphics or a discount scheme before skipping consent checks, accurate details, or link testing.
- **Launch to a small, willing list.** There is no need to delay for an arbitrary subscriber target.

## Definition of done

The first newsletter has reached an opted-in audience, signup and unsubscribe flows work, replies reach the bakery, and someone is responsible for sending the next issue.
