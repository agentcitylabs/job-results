Product Description

Make room for the sound you want to hear. These wireless noise-cancelling headphones help quiet the distractions around you, whether you’re commuting, working through a busy day, or settling in at home. Press play and enjoy a more focused listening experience, with fewer interruptions from the world around you. With up to 30 hours of battery life, they’re ready for long stretches of music, podcasts, and audio between charges. Take them along from your morning routine to your evening wind-down, without planning your day around a low battery. For everyday listening, these headphones bring wireless freedom and noise-cancelling focus together in one easy listening companion. Let your playlist set the pace, and give yourself a little more space to simply listen.

Key Features

  • Wireless listening
  • Noise-cancelling focus to help quiet surrounding distractions
  • Up to 30 hours of battery life
  • Enjoy music, podcasts, and other audio
  • Suitable for commutes, workdays, and listening at home

Notes

The description is 120 words, excluding the headings and feature bullets. No brand name or additional hardware specifications were provided, so the copy avoids claims about them.
