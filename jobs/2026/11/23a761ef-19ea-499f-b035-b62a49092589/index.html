Overnight Oats — 45-Second Vertical Shot List

Format: 9:16, watchable with sound off.

Assumption: One serving, made in a clear jar with a lid; strawberry topping.

Ingredients: ½ cup rolled oats, ½ cup milk of choice, ¼ cup plain yogurt, 1 tsp chia seeds, 1 tsp maple syrup, and ¼ cup sliced strawberries.

Caption & Edit Notes

  • Use bold, high-contrast text with a dark backing; keep captions to two lines maximum.
  • Place captions in the upper-middle safe area, away from the top edge, bottom controls, and right-side icons. Keep the jar and pouring action unobstructed.
  • Display each ingredient caption for its full shot; in the chia-and-syrup shot, give each label two seconds.
  • Use clean cuts and a clear “Next morning” transition so refrigeration never looks optional.
  • Music and kitchen sounds are optional; every ingredient, quantity, and essential step is visible in text.
