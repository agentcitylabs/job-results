Morning Orbit — Logo Concepts

Screen 1 — The Rising Orbit

Idea

A friendly, dependable mark built around the first good moment of the day: coffee rising into view with the sun. The logo feels optimistic and easy to spot from a distance, making it well suited to a compact cart sign, takeaway cups, and social avatars.

Shapes

  • A simple circular sun forms the main icon.
  • One curved orbital line wraps around the sun, suggesting both “orbit” and a curling coffee aroma.
  • A small opening in the orbit keeps the mark from feeling enclosed or overly technical.
  • The wordmark sits beside or beneath the icon in a clean, rounded arrangement.

Two-colour palette

  • Espresso Brown — #3B241C
  • Morning Gold — #F2B84B

Brown gives the brand its coffee grounding, while the golden accent brings warmth and a clear morning cue. The logo should work primarily in brown, with gold used for the sun and small highlights.

Font pairing

  • Wordmark: Fraunces SemiBold
  • Supporting text: DM Sans Regular

Fraunces adds a little character through its soft, editorial serifs without feeling old-fashioned. DM Sans keeps menu boards, prices, and small cart details crisp and readable.

Screen 2 — The Coffee Planet

Idea

A more distinctive, playful direction that treats each cup as its own small world. “Morning Orbit” becomes a cheerful coffee planet: familiar enough to understand immediately, but memorable enough to stand out among standard café logos.

Shapes

  • A rounded coffee cup becomes the central planet shape.
  • A thin elliptical ring passes behind the cup, creating the orbit.
  • The cup’s lid or top edge can subtly double as a sunrise horizon.
  • Two small circular accents suggest moons, beans, or points along a route.
  • The icon can be used independently on stickers, cup sleeves, and a cart flag.

Two-colour palette

  • Deep Navy — #172B3A
  • Cream — #F7E8C6

Deep navy gives the logo strong contrast and all-day usability, while cream keeps it warm and tactile. On the cart, the cream background with navy graphics would feel especially clean and visible.

Font pairing

  • Wordmark: Cooper Black
  • Supporting text: Work Sans Medium

Cooper Black gives the name an approachable, rounded personality that suits a small mobile business. Work Sans balances it with practical, highly legible typography for menus and service information.

Suggested wordmark treatment

Set Morning slightly above Orbit, with the orbit line extending around or underneath the two words. This creates a compact badge that feels made for a cart panel.

Screen 3 — The Espresso Route

Idea

A sharper, more urban concept inspired by the cart’s daily journey through the neighbourhood. The logo combines a coffee bean, a route line, and an orbital path. It feels energetic and modern while still carrying a handmade coffee quality.

Shapes

  • A simplified coffee bean is rotated slightly so it resembles a small planet.
  • A single curved line travels around it like a map route or orbital trail.
  • The route ends in a small dot, representing the cart’s next stop.
  • The bean’s central split can be exaggerated into a subtle crescent shape, linking it to the moon-and-orbit theme.
  • The mark works well as a one-colour stamp on kraft cups and paper bags.

Two-colour palette

  • Charcoal Ink — #242321
  • Terracotta — #C96845

Charcoal provides a strong, practical base for signage and printing. Terracotta introduces the colour of roasted coffee, brick streets, and early sunlight without leaning into a predictable café green.

Font pairing

  • Wordmark: Sora SemiBold
  • Supporting text: Source Sans 3 Regular

Sora’s geometric construction gives the name a confident, contemporary edge. Source Sans 3 is open and easy to read at small sizes, making it useful for menu lettering, opening hours, and social graphics.

Suggested wordmark treatment

Use a slightly spaced-out MORNING ORBIT in uppercase, with the orbiting bean replacing the “O” in Orbit where space allows. For small applications, keep the bean symbol separate from the wordmark to preserve clarity.

Recommended direction

The Rising Orbit is the strongest all-purpose option for Morning Orbit. It communicates coffee and morning immediately, remains legible on a small cart, and gives the business a flexible symbol that can grow across cups, signage, uniforms, and social media.
