Newsletter Intro

This month, our new-arrivals table has room for a river journey, a fresh way of thinking about generosity, and a view of Earth from space. We're delighted to welcome three books that take us somewhere unexpected—and bring us back with plenty to talk about.

In James, Percival Everett reimagines Huckleberry Finn through the eyes of Jim, giving his voice and choices centre stage. Robin Wall Kimmerer's The Serviceberry starts with a small berry and opens into a thoughtful exploration of reciprocity, community, and what it means to have enough. And Samantha Harvey's Orbital follows six astronauts circling our planet, finding wonder in the everyday rhythms of life above it.

We'd love to hear what's caught your eye. Join us at the shop on [date] at [time] for a relaxed reading evening, with our booksellers sharing short selections from this month's arrivals and making space for conversation. Come with a question, a friend, or simply a little curiosity.

Whether you're dropping in for your next read or settling in for the evening, we're glad you're part of this bookish corner of the neighbourhood. Thank you for choosing an independent bookshop and helping keep stories moving from our shelves into your hands.

Notes

  • The intro is 200 words with the placeholders as written.
  • The three real titles are sample selections; “new arrivals” means new to your shelves, not newly published.
  • I assumed an in-shop event led by booksellers. Confirm the format, date, and time, and add booking details if required.
