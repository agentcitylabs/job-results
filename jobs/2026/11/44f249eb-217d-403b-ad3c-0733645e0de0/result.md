# Two-Week Social Media Calendar for a Neighbourhood Gym

| Day | Idea | Format | One-line caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome new and returning members with a quick tour of the gym. | Short Reel | Your neighbourhood gym, your pace—come on in and make yourself at home. |
| 2 | Introduce a coach and share their favourite beginner-friendly exercise. | Photo with caption | Meet [Coach Name]: ask them about [exercise] next time you’re in. |
| 3 | Show a simple warm-up members can do before a workout. | Reel or carousel | Save this easy warm-up for your next session. |
| 4 | Share a member’s story about what they enjoy about training at the gym. | Member photo or quote graphic | “I keep coming back because [member quote].” |
| 5 | Give a quick look at a group class in action. | Short Reel | Good music, good people, and a workout you can make your own. |
| 6 | Post a practical tip for choosing a comfortable workout weight. | Carousel | Start light, focus on good form, and build from there. |
| 7 | Celebrate a small win—showing up, trying a new class, or hitting a personal milestone. | Photo or graphic | Progress isn’t always a big number—it can be simply showing up. |
| 8 | Give a behind-the-scenes look at how the team sets up for the day. | Stories or Reel | Early start, fresh towels, and the doors open for you. |
| 9 | Demonstrate a modification for a common exercise, such as a lower-impact squat. | Reel | There’s more than one way to do a great squat—try the version that suits you. |
| 10 | Highlight a local business or community partner. | Photo or collaborative post | Neighbourhood love: say hello to [Business Name], just around the corner. |
| 11 | Share a short post-workout stretch routine. | Carousel or Reel | Take two minutes to stretch out after your next workout. |
| 12 | Invite members to share their favourite workout song. | Stories poll or question sticker | What song gets you through your last set? Drop it below. |
| 13 | Introduce a piece of gym equipment and explain what it’s used for. | Photo or Reel | Curious about the [equipment]? Ask a coach for a quick walkthrough. |
| 14 | Recap the two weeks with a friendly invitation to visit or try a class. | Photo collage or Reel | Your next workout starts with a first visit—come see what we’re about. |
