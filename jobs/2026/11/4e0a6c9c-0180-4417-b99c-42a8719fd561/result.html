One-Week Launch Plan: Casual Puzzle Game

Assumption: [GAME NAME] is available on iOS and Android at launch. This plan uses organic social posts and placeholders for the store link and game details.

Audience and message

Audience: Casual mobile players who enjoy quick brain teasers, satisfying solutions, and games that fit into a short break.

Core message: [GAME NAME] offers a small, satisfying puzzle to solve whenever you have a few minutes. Keep the tone playful and inviting—not competitive or stressful.

Three channels

  • TikTok: Reach new players with short gameplay clips and a clear puzzle hook.
  • Instagram: Share polished gameplay, launch news, and lightweight audience interaction through Reels and Stories.
  • Reddit: Reach puzzle fans with a transparent, useful post in a relevant community; check each subreddit’s self-promotion rules first.

First-week schedule: five posts

DayChannelPost
1 — LaunchTikTokVideo: Open on a tricky-looking puzzle, show a few seconds of play, then reveal the satisfying solve. Caption: “Can you spot the move? Meet [GAME NAME]—a little puzzle break for your day. Play now: [link]”
1 — LaunchInstagram ReelVideo: A clean gameplay montage with the title and store availability on screen. Caption: “ [GAME NAME] is live! Take a quick break, solve a puzzle, and see how far you get. Download it here: [link]”
3RedditPost: “We’ve just launched [GAME NAME], a casual mobile puzzle game built around short, satisfying solves. We’re the team behind it, and we’d love honest feedback—especially on puzzle difficulty and the first few levels. If you’d like to try it: [link].”
5TikTokVideo: Show one puzzle before the solution; invite viewers to pause and solve it. Caption: “Pause before the reveal—what’s your move? Try more puzzles in [GAME NAME]: [link]”
7Instagram StoriesStory: Post a poll: “What should a puzzle feel like?” Options: “Quick win” / “A real brain-bender.” Follow with a link sticker to the game and a question sticker: “What would you improve?”

What to do during the week

  • Before launch, test the store links and confirm the game page is live; prepare short gameplay clips and post assets.
  • On launch day, publish the TikTok and Reel, then check links and comments.
  • Reply to questions and feedback daily. Share recurring feedback with the game team.
  • On Day 7, review performance and use the poll and comments to choose the next week’s creative angle.

How to measure

Track results daily, using tagged links where available:

  • Discovery: video views, reach, and watch-through rate.
  • Interest: comments, shares, saves, and Reddit replies.
  • Store action: link clicks and store-page visits.
  • Game outcomes: installs, tutorial completion, and Day 1 retention, if analytics are available.

Compare results by channel and post. Use the strongest post’s hook and format as the starting point for the next week; don’t judge success on views alone—look for store visits and installs too.
