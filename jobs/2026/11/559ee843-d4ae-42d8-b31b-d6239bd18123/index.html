Cold Email Sequence: ClinicFlow for Small Clinics

Assumptions: Tool is ClinicFlow (online booking + automated SMS/email reminders for 1–5 provider clinics). Sender is Jordan Hale. Use [First Name] and [Clinic Name] placeholders. Stats reflect typical small-clinic no-show rates (15–25%) and time lost to phone booking. Personalize the first line with one real detail (e.g., a recent Google review or specialty) when possible.

Email 1 — Introduction

Timing: Day 0 (send immediately)

Subject: Empty chairs at [Clinic Name] this week?

Hi [First Name],

Every missed appointment in a small clinic is real money and extra phone time your staff does not have. Most practices your size still lose 1 in 5 slots to no-shows and after-hours calls that never get booked.

ClinicFlow lets patients self-book 24/7 and sends the reminders automatically. Similar clinics cut no-shows 40% in the first 30 days and get staff off the phones.

Would 8 minutes this week to see if it fits [Clinic Name] be useful?

Jordan Hale

ClinicFlow — calendars that actually fill

P.S. Setup takes under an hour. No card needed to try.

Email 2 — Proof + specific result

Timing: 3 business days after Email 1

Subject: How a 3-doctor clinic filled 12 extra slots last week

Hi [First Name],

Quick follow-up. A family practice in Austin was losing eight appointments a week to no-shows and voicemail tag.

They turned on ClinicFlow’s patient booking page and SMS reminders. Result last week: 12 extra filled slots, front desk off the phone, patients booking at 9 p.m.

If [Clinic Name] still handles most scheduling by phone, I can walk you through the exact same setup in under 10 minutes.

Thursday or Friday work for a short call?

Jordan
