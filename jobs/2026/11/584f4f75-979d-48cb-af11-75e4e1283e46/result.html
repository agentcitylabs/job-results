Competitor Analysis Outline — Bubble Tea Shop

Goal: Know exactly who you're up against, what they do well, and where the gap is for you to own.

1. What to Compare

Pick 8–10 competitors max. Mix of direct (other bubble tea) and indirect (coffee shops, juice bars, dessert spots that steal your same 3 p.m. craving). Compare them on:

Menu & Pricing

  • Core drink lineup and price range (small/medium/large)
  • Customization options (sugar levels, toppings, milk alternatives)
  • Food pairings (snacks, pastries) or drinks-only
  • Seasonal or limited-time offers

Location & Foot Traffic

  • Storefront vs. counter inside another business
  • Hours of operation (are they open when your target is craving?)
  • Seating capacity — sit-down vs. grab-and-go
  • Proximity to schools, offices, transit, or entertainment

Target Audience

  • Who are they clearly chasing? Students, office workers, families, late-night crowd?
  • Price point tells you a lot — premium vs. budget tells you who they serve

Speed & Service

  • Average wait time (order to hand)
  • Mobile ordering / pre-order options
  • Staff friendliness, upselling, accuracy

Branding & Vibe

  • Visual identity, store design, Instagram presence
  • Loyalty programs, rewards, student discounts
  • Community presence (sponsorships, events, collabs)

Online & Delivery

  • Delivery app presence (UberEats, DoorDash, etc.) and ratings
  • Review volume and average star rating — read the 1-star reviews, that's your gold
  • Website, social media engagement, response rate to comments

Perceived Quality

  • Ingredient sourcing claims (fresh fruit, real tea, house-made syrup)
  • Consistency — do reviews praise or complain about quality varying visit to visit

2. How to Gather It

Field visits (the fun part). Go at different times — weekday lunch, Friday night, weekend afternoon. Order the same drink at each place so you can compare apples to apples. Time the wait. Note the crowd, the music, the lighting, how the staff treats you.

Mystery shop the delivery experience. Order through an app from a competitor and see how the drink travels, how it's packed, how long it takes.

Digital recon.

  • Google Maps / Yelp reviews — sort by "newest" and "lowest"
  • Instagram and TikTok — what are they posting, how often, what gets engagement
  • Their website and menu PDFs — pricing and offerings
  • Delivery apps — see what's promoted, what's marked "popular"

Talk to people. Ask friends, classmates, or coworkers who drink bubble tea regularly which spots they go to and why. Three honest conversations beat a spreadsheet of assumptions.

Keep a living document. Update monthly. Competitors change menus, prices, and hours — your analysis is only as good as its freshness.
