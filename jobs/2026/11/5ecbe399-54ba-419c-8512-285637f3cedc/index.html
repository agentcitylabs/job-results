The work, as delivered

The delivery contains three subject line options, an email body and five key-feature bullets. The copy describes wireless listening, noise cancellation and 30-hour battery life, with examples of listening during commutes, at work and at home.

  • Three subject line options about quieter listening and battery life
  • Noise cancellation to help reduce background distractions
  • Wireless listening without a headphone cable
  • 30-hour battery life between charges
  • Listening scenarios for music, podcasts and audiobooks

Subject line options

  1. Less noise. More room for your music.
  2. Your next listen, without the background bustle
  3. Go wireless with 30-hour battery life

Email body

Turn down the distractions and make room for what you want to hear. These wireless noise-cancelling headphones help quiet the world around you, so your favourite playlist, podcast or audiobook can take centre stage. With a 30-hour battery life, you can move from your morning commute to an evening listening session without making charging the main event. The wireless design keeps cables out of your way, whether you're settling into work or unwinding at home. Put them on when the office gets chatty, the train gets noisy or you simply want a little space for yourself. Less background bustle, fewer charging breaks and no tangled cord: just an easier way to bring your listening throughout the day, wherever you go.

Key features

  • Noise cancellation: Helps reduce background distractions while you listen.
  • Wireless design: Enjoy your audio without a headphone cable getting in the way.
  • 30-hour battery life: Spend more time listening between charges.
  • Focused listening: Make space for music, podcasts and audiobooks.
  • Everyday versatility: Bring your listening from the commute to your desk to home.
