# 10-Slide Pitch Deck Outline  
## App: Local Dog-Walking Marketplace

### Slide 1 — **Better Walks for Happier Dogs**
**Key point:** Introduce the app as a trusted way for dog owners to find, book, and manage reliable local dog walkers.

### Slide 2 — **Dog Owners Struggle to Find Reliable Help**
**Key point:** Busy schedules, long workdays, travel, and unexpected commitments make it difficult for owners to arrange consistent, trustworthy dog care.

### Slide 3 — **Existing Options Are Fragmented and Inconvenient**
**Key point:** Owners often rely on personal referrals, informal arrangements, or broad pet-care platforms that may lack local availability, transparency, and consistent quality.

### Slide 4 — **A Simple Local Match, Made for Dogs**
**Key point:** The app matches dog owners with nearby walkers based on location, schedule, dog needs, experience, and preferences.

### Slide 5 — **How It Works**
**Key point:**  
1. Owners create a dog profile and walking request.  
2. The app recommends suitable local walkers.  
3. Owners compare profiles, reviews, availability, and pricing.  
4. They book, pay, and receive walk updates in one place.

### Slide 6 — **Trust Is Built Into Every Booking**
**Key point:** Verification, background checks, reviews, secure payments, real-time updates, and emergency contact details help owners feel confident about who is caring for their dog.

### Slide 7 — **A Better Experience for Walkers, Too**
**Key point:** Dog walkers gain access to nearby clients, flexible scheduling, clear earnings, repeat bookings, and tools to manage their walking business.

### Slide 8 — **A Large and Recurring Market**
**Key point:** The app serves dog owners who need regular or occasional walks, while recurring bookings create frequent usage and predictable revenue opportunities.

### Slide 9 — **Business Model and Growth**
**Key point:** Generate revenue through a service fee on each booking, with potential additional income from subscriptions, premium visibility for walkers, and partnerships with pet-care brands and local businesses.

### Slide 10 — **Making Local Dog Care More Dependable**
**Key point:** Close with the vision: become the trusted local network for dog walking, starting in one market and expanding city by city through strong matches, repeat bookings, and word-of-mouth growth.
