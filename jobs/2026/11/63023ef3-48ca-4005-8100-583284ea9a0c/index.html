The work, as delivered

The delivered script includes an opening, five discovery questions and a closing section. It asks how the restaurant manages its website, what problems it faces and which tasks it would like to hand off. The close proposes a website review before recommending a maintenance plan, with an alternative response for prospects who are not ready.

  • Opening checks whether it is a good time to talk and explains the call’s purpose.
  • Questions cover website ownership and updates to menus, hours and prices.
  • Questions explore guest actions, recent website problems and tasks to hand off.
  • Close reflects the restaurant’s stated priority and proposes a website review.
  • Fallback offers a brief summary if the prospect is not ready.

Website Maintenance Discovery Call Script

Opening

“Hi, [Name] — this is [Your Name] from [Company]. We help small restaurants keep their websites current and working smoothly. Is now still a good time for a quick conversation?”

“Great. I’d like to learn how you manage your site today, what matters most to the restaurant, and whether ongoing maintenance could make things easier. No pressure if it’s not a fit.”

Five questions

  1. “Who looks after the website when something needs updating or fixing?”
  1. “How often do details like your menu, hours, prices, or holiday openings change—and how easy is it to update them?”
  1. “Does the website help guests take actions that matter to you, such as calling, making a reservation, placing an order, or finding directions?”
  1. “Have you had any recent website problems—like outdated information, broken links, slow pages, or difficulty making changes?”
  1. “If you could hand off one website task or worry, what would you most want someone to take care of?”

Close

“Thanks—that gives me a good picture. Based on what you’ve shared, it sounds like [briefly reflect their need, such as ‘keeping the menu and hours current’ or ‘having someone to call when the site has a problem’] is the main priority.”

“If you’d like, the next step is a short review of your website. I can then recommend a maintenance plan based on what it actually needs and explain the cost before you decide. Would [day/time] work?”

If they’re not ready:

“Of course. I can send a brief summary of what we discussed, and you can reach out if maintaining the site becomes a priority. Thanks for your time, [Name]—and good luck with [specific detail they mentioned].”
