Summary

A 45-second, 9:16 storyboard for one serving of overnight oats.

It includes two opening-hook options, timed shots, framing, and ready-to-use on-screen captions.

Use either hook for the opening three seconds; the rest of the edit stays the same.

Storyboard

Format: Vertical 9:16 · Runtime: 45 seconds · Serving: 1

TimeShot and framingOn-screen caption
0–3 sec — Hook ATight macro: spoon lifts a thick, berry-topped bite from the jar. Hold for the texture.Breakfast, ready when you are.
0–3 sec — Hook BOverhead: slide a finished jar into frame beside a spoon; quick push-in.Make tomorrow’s breakfast tonight.
3–7 secOverhead ingredient lineup on a clean counter. Keep labels or small amounts of each ingredient visible.For 1 serving: ½ cup rolled oats · 1 tbsp chia seeds · ½ cup milk · ¼ cup Greek yogurt · 1 tsp maple syrup · pinch of salt
7–12 secClose overhead: pour oats into a clear jar. Let them fall into frame.Add ½ cup rolled oats
12–17 secClose-up: sprinkle in chia seeds and salt; show the layers through the glass.Add 1 tbsp chia + a pinch of salt
17–22 secSide close-up: add milk, yogurt, and maple syrup. Briefly pause so each ingredient reads on camera.Pour in milk, yogurt + maple syrup
22–27 secTop-down: stir until evenly combined, scraping the bottom once.Stir until combined
27–31 secMedium close-up: seal the jar, then place it in the fridge.Cover and chill overnight
31–35 secMatch cut: open the fridge and take out the jar. Optional quick cut to the lid coming off.In the morning…
35–40 secClose-up: add berries on top; finish with a small sprinkle of nuts or seeds if desired.Top with berries (and a little crunch)
40–45 secHero shot: spoon dips in, lifts a bite, then set the jar beside the spoon.Stir, top, enjoy.

Notes

  • The overnight chill is represented by a fridge cut; film the morning shots after the oats have chilled for at least 6 hours.
  • Captions assume a standard single-serving jar and unsweetened milk. Adjust the maple syrup or toppings to taste.
  • Hook captions are alternatives, not consecutive lines.
