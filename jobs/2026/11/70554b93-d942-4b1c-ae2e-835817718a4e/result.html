SOP: New Hire’s First Day

Purpose: Give every new hire a welcoming, consistent first day and the access and context they need to begin work safely.

Applies to: All new employees at the agency.

Owners: The new hire’s manager coordinates the day; People/Operations handles paperwork and logistics.

First-day steps

WhenStepOwner
Before arrivalConfirm the start time, location or video link, schedule, and who will greet the new hire. Prepare their workspace, computer, accounts, and any required paperwork.People/Operations
9:00Welcome the new hire, show them around, introduce nearby teammates, and explain practical details such as breaks, lunch, and building access. For remote hires, give a brief virtual orientation.Manager or assigned buddy
9:30Complete employment forms and confirm payroll, emergency contact, and any required policy acknowledgments. Share where to find the employee handbook and agency policies.People/Operations
10:15Set up and test the computer, email, messaging, calendar, file storage, project-management platform, and design tools needed for the role. Confirm access is appropriate to the hire’s work.IT or designated operations owner
11:00Review account security, password and MFA requirements, device care, file handling, and client confidentiality. Explain how to report a security concern.IT or Operations
11:30Give an overview of the agency: services, team structure, current priorities, working norms, and how projects move from brief to delivery.Manager
12:30Have lunch with the team or buddy. Keep it informal and make sure the new hire knows whom to contact with questions.Buddy or manager
1:30Walk through the role’s responsibilities, near-term goals, communication preferences, and how feedback and approvals work. Share the relevant project folders and templates.Manager
2:30Arrange introductory meetings with key collaborators and explain who handles common requests, such as resourcing, expenses, IT support, and client questions.Manager
3:30Give the new hire a small, low-risk first task or guided practice exercise. Confirm the brief, deadline, and review contact; do not assign unsupervised client-critical work on day one.Manager
4:30Check in: ask what is clear or confusing, resolve immediate blockers, recap the next day’s plan, and confirm the new hire has everything needed to leave or log off comfortably.Manager

Adjust the times to fit the agency’s schedule. For remote hires, replace in-person tours and introductions with video calls and links to shared resources.

First-day checklist

People/Operations

  • [ ] Confirm start details, schedule, and welcome contact.
  • [ ] Prepare workspace, equipment, and required forms.
  • [ ] Complete employment and policy paperwork.
  • [ ] Share the handbook and explain how to get help.

IT/Operations

  • [ ] Provide the computer and required accessories.
  • [ ] Set up and test role-appropriate accounts and tools.
  • [ ] Confirm MFA and basic security guidance.
  • [ ] Explain how to request support and report a security issue.

Manager or buddy

  • [ ] Welcome the new hire and make introductions.
  • [ ] Explain agency structure, working norms, and project workflow.
  • [ ] Review role expectations and near-term goals.
  • [ ] Share relevant folders, templates, and communication channels.
  • [ ] Assign a suitable first task with a clear brief and reviewer.
  • [ ] Hold an end-of-day check-in and confirm tomorrow’s plan.

New hire

  • [ ] Complete required forms and acknowledgments.
  • [ ] Sign in to the tools needed for the role.
  • [ ] Ask questions and note any access or equipment issues.
  • [ ] Confirm the next day’s start time and priorities.
