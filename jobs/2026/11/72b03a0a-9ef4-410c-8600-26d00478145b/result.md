# Coffee Shop Customer Satisfaction Survey

*Thinking about your visit today, please answer the following.*

| # | Question | Answer type |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Overall, how satisfied were you with your visit? | **5-point scale:** Very dissatisfied / Dissatisfied / Neither / Satisfied / Very satisfied |
| 2 | How satisfied were you with the quality of your coffee or other items? | **5-point scale** as above, plus **Not applicable** |
| 3 | Was your order prepared correctly? | **Yes / No / Not applicable** |
| 4 | How satisfied were you with the time it took to receive your order? | **5-point scale** as above |
| 5 | How satisfied were you with the friendliness of our staff? | **5-point scale** as above |
| 6 | How satisfied were you with the comfort and atmosphere of the shop? | **5-point scale** as above, plus **Not applicable** |
| 7 | How satisfied were you with the value for money? | **5-point scale** as above |
| 8 | What’s one thing we could do to improve your next visit? | **Open text** |

## How to read the results

- **Track the share satisfied:** For each 5-point question, count “Satisfied” and “Very satisfied” responses as positive. Also keep the full rating distribution so you can see whether responses are neutral or strongly negative.
- **Find the biggest opportunities:** Compare positive-response rates across questions. The lowest-rated areas point to where improvements may have the most impact; review those alongside written comments.
- **Watch order accuracy separately:** Report the percentage of “Yes” answers among applicable responses, and look for recurring reasons behind “No” answers in the comments.
- **Group open-text answers into themes:** For example, wait times, drink consistency, seating, or staff interactions. Note how often each theme appears, and include a few representative comments.
- **Use the overall rating as your headline measure:** Review it over time and alongside the other questions to see whether changes in specific parts of the visit correspond with overall satisfaction.
- **Include response counts:** Show how many customers answered each question. Treat small samples cautiously, and compare results over similar periods or visit types.
