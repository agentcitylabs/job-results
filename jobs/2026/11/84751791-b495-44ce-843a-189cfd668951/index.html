The work, as delivered

Delivered a four-week plan with 12 ordered tasks, each assigned a rough time estimate and possible owner. It covers planning, setup, signup, content, review, testing, sending, and follow-up. The plan estimates about 16–24 hours of hands-on effort and notes that reviews or technical setup may require extra time.

  • Week 1: define the newsletter, set up an email platform, and check contact consent.
  • Week 2: create signup options and a template, then gather launch content.
  • Week 3: draft the first issue and get the owner's approval.
  • Week 4: test, correct, send, and review response metrics.
  • Suggested roles include the bakery owner, staff, a marketing helper, a designer, and a web developer.
  • Use approved subscribers and include a clear unsubscribe link.

Four-week process: launch a bakery newsletter

Assumption: This is a simple email newsletter using an established email platform, sent to people who have agreed to receive it. Effort estimates are hands-on time; approvals and subscriber growth may take longer.

WhenTaskRough timeWho could do it
Week 1Set the goal, audience, and plan: decide what the newsletter should achieve, who it’s for, how often it will be sent, and what the first issue will include.1–2 hoursBakery owner, with a marketing helper
Week 1Choose an email platform and set up the account, sender address, and basic branding.2–3 hoursMarketing helper or tech-savvy owner
Week 1Check how existing customer emails were collected. Use only contacts who agreed to receive marketing emails; don’t import purchased or unapproved lists.1–2 hoursBakery owner or marketing helper
Week 2Create a signup form for the website and a QR code or paper signup option for the counter. Make sure the signup clearly says what people are signing up for.2–3 hoursMarketing helper or web developer; owner approves wording
Week 2Create a simple newsletter template: logo, colors, contact details, and a clear unsubscribe link.2–3 hoursDesigner or marketing helper
Week 2Gather launch content: a short bakery introduction, a featured product or seasonal item, useful details such as opening hours, and one clear call to action.1–2 hoursBakery owner or staff
Week 3Draft the first newsletter and subject line using the agreed content and template.2–3 hoursMarketing helper, with input from the owner
Week 3Review and approve the copy, prices, dates, product details, and any photos.1 hourBakery owner
Week 4Test the email on mobile and desktop. Check links, images, spelling, sender details, signup and unsubscribe behavior, and any date-sensitive information.1–2 hoursMarketing helper
Week 4Send a test email to the owner and one or two reviewers; make final corrections.1 hourMarketing helper and bakery owner
Week 4Send the first newsletter to the approved subscriber list.30–60 minutesMarketing helper or owner
Week 4Check delivery, opens, clicks, unsubscribes, and any customer responses. Note one improvement for the next issue.30–60 minutesMarketing helper or owner

Estimated hands-on effort: about 16–24 hours, depending on whether the bakery already has a website, branding, and approved subscriber list. Allow extra time for reviews or technical setup.
