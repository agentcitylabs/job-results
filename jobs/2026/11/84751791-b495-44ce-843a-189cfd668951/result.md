# Four-week process: launch a bakery newsletter

**Assumption:** This is a simple email newsletter using an established email platform, sent to people who have agreed to receive it. Effort estimates are hands-on time; approvals and subscriber growth may take longer.

| When | Task | Rough time | Who could do it |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Week 1** | Set the goal, audience, and plan: decide what the newsletter should achieve, who it’s for, how often it will be sent, and what the first issue will include. | 1–2 hours | Bakery owner, with a marketing helper |
| **Week 1** | Choose an email platform and set up the account, sender address, and basic branding. | 2–3 hours | Marketing helper or tech-savvy owner |
| **Week 1** | Check how existing customer emails were collected. Use only contacts who agreed to receive marketing emails; don’t import purchased or unapproved lists. | 1–2 hours | Bakery owner or marketing helper |
| **Week 2** | Create a signup form for the website and a QR code or paper signup option for the counter. Make sure the signup clearly says what people are signing up for. | 2–3 hours | Marketing helper or web developer; owner approves wording |
| **Week 2** | Create a simple newsletter template: logo, colors, contact details, and a clear unsubscribe link. | 2–3 hours | Designer or marketing helper |
| **Week 2** | Gather launch content: a short bakery introduction, a featured product or seasonal item, useful details such as opening hours, and one clear call to action. | 1–2 hours | Bakery owner or staff |
| **Week 3** | Draft the first newsletter and subject line using the agreed content and template. | 2–3 hours | Marketing helper, with input from the owner |
| **Week 3** | Review and approve the copy, prices, dates, product details, and any photos. | 1 hour | Bakery owner |
| **Week 4** | Test the email on mobile and desktop. Check links, images, spelling, sender details, signup and unsubscribe behavior, and any date-sensitive information. | 1–2 hours | Marketing helper |
| **Week 4** | Send a test email to the owner and one or two reviewers; make final corrections. | 1 hour | Marketing helper and bakery owner |
| **Week 4** | Send the first newsletter to the approved subscriber list. | 30–60 minutes | Marketing helper or owner |
| **Week 4** | Check delivery, opens, clicks, unsubscribes, and any customer responses. Note one improvement for the next issue. | 30–60 minutes | Marketing helper or owner |

**Estimated hands-on effort:** about **16–24 hours**, depending on whether the bakery already has a website, branding, and approved subscriber list. Allow extra time for reviews or technical setup.
