# 15-Second Language-Learning App Ad Scripts

## Version 1: The “Missed Moment” Hook

**Best suited for:** Travelers, expats, and people preparing for an upcoming trip.

**0:00–0:03**  
**Visual:** A traveler smiles at a café counter, but the server speaks quickly in another language. The traveler freezes.  
**VO:** “Ever had the perfect thing to say… five minutes too late?”

**0:03–0:08**  
**Visual:** Cut to the traveler using **[App Name]** on their phone. A short lesson teaches the exact café phrase.  
**VO:** “With [App Name], learn useful phrases in quick, everyday lessons.”

**0:08–0:12**  
**Visual:** The traveler confidently orders in the local language. The server smiles.  
**Traveler:** “One coffee, please.”  
**VO:** “So you’re ready when the moment arrives.”

**0:12–0:15**  
**Visual:** App logo and download prompt.  
**VO:** “Start speaking with [App Name]. Download now.”

## Version 2: The “Tiny Habit” Hook

**Best suited for:** Busy adults, students, and beginners who feel they don’t have time to learn a language.

**0:00–0:03**  
**Visual:** A person opens a language textbook, sees a huge stack of pages, and slowly closes it.  
**VO:** “Think you need an hour a day to learn a language?”

**0:03–0:08**  
**Visual:** They switch to **[App Name]** during a short break. A simple lesson begins.  
**VO:** “You don’t. [App Name] gives you bite-sized practice that fits into real life.”

**0:08–0:12**  
**Visual:** Quick cuts: learning on a train, waiting for coffee, and before bed. A progress streak grows.  
**VO:** “A few minutes today can become real progress tomorrow.”

**0:12–0:15**  
**Visual:** App logo, cheerful progress screen, and download prompt.  
**VO:** “Make learning a habit with [App Name]. Download now.”
