# Neighbourhood Gym: Two-Week Content Calendar

**Assumption:** This plan covers one post each weekday for two weeks—10 posts total, matching the package. Weekends are left open.

| Day | Post idea | Format | One-line caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Week 1 · Monday** | **Your first visit, minus the guesswork:** Show the entrance, check-in point and workout floor so newcomers know what to expect. | Short walkthrough Reel | “New here? Here’s the route from our front door to your first workout—no guesswork needed.” |
| **Week 1 · Tuesday** | **Meet a familiar face:** Introduce a trainer with their name, favourite exercise and one thing they help beginners learn. | Staff portrait with text overlay | “Meet [Name]—your friendly face for equipment questions and finding a comfortable starting point.” |
| **Week 1 · Wednesday** | **One machine, explained:** Have a trainer demonstrate how to adjust and use a commonly misunderstood machine. | Three-slide carousel: adjust, position, move | “That machine you keep walking past? Let’s make it a little less mysterious.” |
| **Week 1 · Thursday** | **The win that doesn’t need a scale:** With permission, spotlight a member’s milestone, such as their first full month of consistent visits. | Member photo and short story | “A month of showing up deserves a shout-out—nice work, [Name].” |
| **Week 1 · Friday** | **Let the neighbourhood choose:** Ask followers which equipment tutorial they want next: cable machine or rowing machine. | Two-frame Story with poll | “What should we explain next: the cable machine or the rower? You pick.” |
| **Week 2 · Monday** | **Short on time? Start here:** Ask a trainer to demonstrate a simple 20-minute session, including a warm-up and easier options. | Saveable workout carousel | “Only got 20 minutes? Save this simple session and work at a pace that suits you.” |
| **Week 2 · Tuesday** | **A quieter time to try us:** Use actual attendance patterns to highlight a typically less-busy window, noting that it can vary. | Gym-floor photo with time overlay | “Prefer a little more breathing room? [Day/time] is usually quieter—ask us before you head over.” |
| **Week 2 · Wednesday** | **Small details, shared space:** Show how to wipe equipment, return weights and ask to work in between sets. | Friendly demonstration Reel | “Wipe it down, put it back, say hello—the little things make this gym feel neighbourly.” |
| **Week 2 · Thursday** | **You asked, we’re showing you:** Publish the equipment tutorial chosen in last Friday’s poll, with setup guidance from a trainer. | Follow-up Reel | “You voted for [equipment]—here’s how to get set up for your first try.” |
| **Week 2 · Friday** | **Celebrate the small wins:** Invite followers to share something they learned or felt proud of this week, without making it a competition. | Question-card post | “Found your way around, tried something new or simply turned up? Tell us your win this week.” |

**Before publishing:** Replace bracketed details, verify the quieter-time recommendation and get permission from anyone featured.
