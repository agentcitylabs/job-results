## 15-Second Ad Scripts

### 1. For travelers
**Hook A:** “Your next trip deserves more than pointing.”  
**Hook B:** “What if ordering dinner abroad felt easy?”

**Body:** Open the app, learn a useful phrase, then say it out loud with a quick speaking practice. A few minutes today can make your next conversation feel more natural. **Start learning now.**

### 2. For busy professionals
**Hook A:** “No hour to study? No problem.”  
**Hook B:** “Make your commute a mini language lesson.”

**Body:** Fit a quick lesson into a coffee break or your ride home. Practice practical phrases, build a steady habit, and keep making progress in small steps. **Try a lesson today.**

### 3. For hesitant beginners
**Hook A:** “Nervous about getting the words wrong?”  
**Hook B:** “You don’t have to be fluent to start speaking.”

**Body:** Learn one phrase at a time, then practice saying it in the app before you use it in real life. Start small, find your rhythm, and keep going. **Begin today.**
