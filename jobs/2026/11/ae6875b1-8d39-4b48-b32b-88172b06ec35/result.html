Morning Orbit — Logo Concepts

Three distinct directions for a small coffee cart, each designed to stay clear on a menu board, cup stamp, or cart sign.

1. First Light

Idea: Morning coffee as the first glow of the day. A warm, simple mark that feels welcoming from across the street.

Shapes: A rising half-sun sits behind a small coffee cup; the cup’s steam forms a single curved ray. Keep the silhouette bold, with no fine details, so it holds up at small sizes.

Two-colour palette:

  • Espresso — #3B261E
  • Dawn Gold — #E6A84A

Font pairing: Fraunces SemiBold for “Morning” + DM Sans Medium for “ORBIT” in small caps. The rounded serif adds warmth; the clean sans keeps the name crisp on signage.

2. The Daily Loop

Idea: A familiar morning ritual, repeated with pleasure. The orbit becomes a circular route around the cart—and around your daily coffee.

Shapes: A clean circular ring wraps around a compact takeaway cup. A small dot on the ring suggests a planet or a point on the cart’s route. Set the name in a neat horizontal lockup beside or beneath the icon.

Two-colour palette:

  • Night Roast — #292A35
  • Oat Milk — #F2E7D2

Font pairing: Space Grotesk Medium for “Morning Orbit” + Space Grotesk Regular for supporting text. One family keeps the mark tidy and modern, while the geometric letterforms echo the orbital ring.

3. Little Planet Coffee

Idea: A playful, memorable cart identity with a small universe of its own—bright, friendly, and easy to spot in a busy market.

Shapes: A round planet-shaped coffee cup with a saucer-like orbit crossing behind it. Add one small star as an accent; keep the orbit open so the mark feels light rather than enclosed.

Two-colour palette:

  • Deep Teal — #174B4A
  • Tangerine — #F28C52

Font pairing: Cooper Black for “Morning” + Montserrat Medium for “ORBIT.” The expressive headline brings character; the simple companion line keeps the full name easy to read.
