The work, as delivered

The delivered shot list contains 10 timed shots spanning 45 seconds in vertical 9:16 format. Each shot includes framing, action and movement, and an on-screen caption. It assumes no voiceover and a warm kitchen setting, with additional notes on caption placement, typography, motion and an optional animated jar illustration.

  • Shot timings run from the opening oat pour to a final fridge shot.
  • Framing includes overhead, eye-level, side-profile, macro and low-angle views.
  • Recipe captions specify quantities for oats, milk, Greek yogurt and chia seeds.
  • Actions include pouring, adding toppings, shaking the jar and lifting a spoonful.
  • Styling notes cover caption placement, a bold sans-serif font and a honey-colored accent.
  • Motion guidance suggests slower pours and a shake timed to the music.

Shot List: Overnight Oats (45 seconds, vertical 9:16)

Working assumptions: no voiceover, captions carry the recipe, warm morning light, cozy kitchen mood. All timings are approximate — feel free to stretch or snip each by half a second.

A few extra notes to sell it:

  • Keep captions in the lower safe area, about 15% from the bottom — nothing snags on the phone’s camera island.
  • Use one clean sans-serif, bold, white with a slight shadow. Let the honey shot caption have a little honey-colored underline, but that’s the only decorative accent.
  • Motion is the star: slow every pour just a touch, shake on the beat, and let the spoon pull linger the longest. That’s the money shot.
  • End with a small animated jar illustration beside the last caption, if you want to carry the brand through.

That’s the complete short — warm, quick, and it makes you want overnight oats immediately.
