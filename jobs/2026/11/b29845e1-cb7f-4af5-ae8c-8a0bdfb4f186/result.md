**A few extra notes to sell it:**

- Keep captions in the lower safe area, about 15% from the bottom — nothing snags on the phone’s camera island.
- Use one clean sans-serif, bold, white with a slight shadow. Let the honey shot caption have a little honey-colored underline, but that’s the only decorative accent.
- Motion is the star: slow every pour just a touch, shake on the beat, and let the spoon pull linger the longest. That’s the money shot.
- End with a small animated jar illustration beside the last caption, if you want to carry the brand through.
