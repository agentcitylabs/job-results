# Morning Orbit — Logo Concepts

Three shelf-ready directions for a compact coffee cart identity. Each is designed to stay legible on a cart sign, takeaway cup and small social avatar.

## 1. First Light
**Idea:** A warm, everyday wake-up: the sun rising over the rim of a coffee cup. Friendly and immediately readable.

**Shapes:** A simple half-circle sun sits above a low, shallow cup curve. Two short rays add a sunrise cue without clutter. Keep the whole mark within a circle for easy use as a badge.

**Two-colour palette:**  
- Espresso — `#3B2923`  
- Buttermilk — `#F4D99B`

**Font pairing:** **Fraunces SemiBold** for “Morning Orbit” + **DM Sans** for small supporting text. The soft serif adds character; the sans stays clear at small sizes.

## 2. Daily Orbit
**Idea:** A more distinctive, modern mark that turns “Orbit” into the hero: a coffee bean travelling around a cup-inspired planet.

**Shapes:** One clean circular orbit loops around a small bean-shaped oval, with a short break in the line to suggest motion. Pair it with a simple wordmark; avoid tiny coffee details that disappear on cups.

**Two-colour palette:**  
- Deep teal — `#164A45`  
- Warm cream — `#F5E9D0`

**Font pairing:** **Space Grotesk Medium** for “Morning Orbit” + **Source Sans 3** for supporting text. Geometric but approachable, with strong readability on signage.

## 3. The Little Cart
**Idea:** A local, hand-made feel that makes the cart itself part of the identity—like a familiar neighbourhood stop.

**Shapes:** A compact front-on cart silhouette with a striped awning; a small circle above it doubles as a rising sun and an orbit. Use chunky, simplified shapes so the illustration holds up on a stamp.

**Two-colour palette:**  
- Brick red — `#B94F3B`  
- Oat — `#F2E5CC`

**Font pairing:** **Bree Serif** for “Morning Orbit” + **Nunito Sans** for supporting text. The rounded letterforms suit the friendly illustration without feeling childish.

## Top 10 design issues, ranked

1. **Small-size legibility:** Simplify the mark so it still reads on cup stamps and profile icons.  
2. **Name clarity:** Keep “Morning Orbit” prominent; don’t let the illustration overpower it.  
3. **Distinctiveness:** Avoid relying on a generic cup-and-steam symbol alone.  
4. **One-colour use:** Make sure each concept also works in a single ink for low-cost printing.  
5. **Signage distance:** Test the wordmark from a few metres away before settling on letter spacing or weight.  
6. **Production limits:** Use solid shapes and sturdy lines that work for stickers, vinyl and rubber stamps.  
7. **Palette contrast:** Preserve clear text contrast, especially on cream backgrounds in bright daylight.  
8. **Illustration complexity:** Keep details sparse; fine rays, stripes or orbit lines can fill in when reproduced small.  
9. **Font availability:** Confirm the chosen fonts are licensed and available for the cart’s web and print needs.  
10. **Future flexibility:** Leave room for seasonal drinks or location details without changing the core logo.
