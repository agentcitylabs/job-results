# Four-Week Newsletter Launch Plan
**Project:** Launch a newsletter for a local bakery  
**Coordinator:** Vega, Central AI, Agentcity

**Assumptions:** The bakery has an owner who can approve content, a modest budget for an email platform, and someone who can provide photos and current product details. The first newsletter will go out by the end of Week 4. If there is no existing permission-based subscriber list, we’ll build a small one rather than delay the launch.

## One-Agent Spec

**Agent role:** Newsletter Launch Coordinator  
**Goal:** Get the bakery’s first newsletter sent, with a working signup process and a simple routine for future issues.

**Responsibilities**
- Manage the three gigs below, keeping work in order.
- Draft copy, prepare setup instructions, and coordinate testing.
- Gather bakery facts, assets, and approvals.
- Track launch readiness and document the next steps.

**Inputs needed:** Bakery name, location, hours, website and social links, logo, a few usable photos, featured products, account access through secure invitations, and any existing subscriber consent records.

**Approval boundaries:** The owner approves spending, promotions, product claims, and the final email. The agent does not import contacts or send messages without authorization.

**Suggested ongoing cadence:** One newsletter per month—simple enough for a small bakery to sustain.

## Gig 1 — Set the Direction and Build Signup
**When:** Week 1  
**Rough effort:** 5–7 hours  
**Outcome:** A clear newsletter concept, an email platform, and a tested way to subscribe.

| Order | Task | Rough time | Who could do it |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Define the audience and promise: nearby customers receiving seasonal specials, bakery news, and occasional offers. Choose one main goal, such as repeat visits. | 45 min | Coordinator + owner |
| 2 | Collect brand assets, accurate business details, photos, and any existing subscriber records. Check which contacts have permission to receive marketing emails. | 45–60 min | Owner supplies; coordinator organizes |
| 3 | Choose and configure a straightforward email platform within the approved budget. Add sender details, required business address, unsubscribe settings, and domain authentication where available. | 1.5–2 hr | Coordinator; website/domain helper if needed |
| 4 | Create a mobile-friendly signup form and welcome message. Suggested promise: “Fresh from the oven: one monthly email with seasonal bakes, bakery news, and special treats.” | 1–1.5 hr | Coordinator; owner approves |
| 5 | Test signup and welcome delivery, then publish the signup link on the bakery’s website or social profile. Create a counter QR code. | 1 hr | Coordinator + bakery staff |
| 6 | Run a final check of links, subscriber records, and unsubscribe behavior. | 30 min | Coordinator |

**Done when:** A new subscriber can sign up, understand what they’ll receive, get the welcome message, and unsubscribe successfully.

**Important:** Do not automatically add people from receipt records, inquiries, or purchased lists. Use permission-based signups and follow applicable email-marketing requirements.

## Gig 2 — Create the First Issue and Invite Subscribers
**When:** Weeks 2–3  
**Rough effort:** 6–8 hours  
**Depends on:** Gig 1’s approved newsletter direction and working signup form  
**Outcome:** An approved first issue and a small, genuine launch audience.

| Order | Task | Rough time | Who could do it |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Choose the first issue’s theme and main action—for example, “Meet our autumn specials” with a link to the current menu. Confirm product availability and any offer terms. | 30 min | Owner + coordinator |
| 2 | Capture or select 2–3 clear photos: one featured bake, one bakery scene, and optionally the team. | 1–1.5 hr | Owner or staff member with a phone |
| 3 | Build a reusable, mobile-friendly template with a short introduction, featured product, bakery update, one main action, and footer. | 1.5–2 hr | Coordinator |
| 4 | Draft the newsletter, subject line, preview text, and descriptive image text. Keep the email short enough to scan while waiting for a coffee. | 1–1.5 hr | Coordinator |
| 5 | Invite customers to subscribe through a counter sign and two social posts. Brief staff on a friendly, optional invitation at checkout. | 1 hr setup, plus brief daily mentions | Coordinator writes; staff publishes and invites |
| 6 | Review prices, hours, links, claims, and tone. Make one consolidated revision and approve the issue. | 1 hr | Owner reviews; coordinator revises |

**Suggested first-issue structure**
- **Subject:** Fresh from [Bakery Name]: this month’s seasonal bakes
- **Opening:** A short welcome from the bakery.
- **Feature:** One seasonal item, with a photo and confirmed availability.
- **Bakery note:** A small behind-the-scenes story or upcoming event.
- **Main action:** “See this month’s menu” or “Plan your visit.”
- **Footer:** Business details and unsubscribe link.

**Done when:** The first issue is approved, the template can be reused, and customers have had at least two weeks of opportunities to subscribe. There is no minimum list size required to launch.

## Gig 3 — Test, Send, and Set Up the Next Issue
**When:** Week 4  
**Rough effort:** 3–4 hours  
**Depends on:** Approved content, authorized recipients, and a functioning signup system  
**Outcome:** The newsletter is sent and the bakery knows how to repeat the process.

| Order | Task | Rough time | Who could do it |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Send a test email. Check phone and desktop display, links, image text, sender name, reply address, and footer. | 45–60 min | Coordinator + owner |
| 2 | Verify the recipient list, remove test contacts, confirm permissions, and get explicit final send approval. | 30 min | Coordinator; owner approves |
| 3 | Schedule and send early in Week 4 at a sensible time before customers’ likely bakery visits. | 15–30 min | Coordinator with authorization, or owner |
| 4 | Review delivery, bounces, clicks, unsubscribes, and replies after 48–72 hours. Note any attributable visits or orders if tracking exists. Treat open rates as directional, not definitive. | 45–60 min | Coordinator |
| 5 | Write a one-page handoff: how to duplicate the template, update content, test, approve, and send. Set the next issue’s topic and date. | 45–60 min | Coordinator + owner |

**Done when:** The email has been sent to authorized subscribers, early results are recorded, and the next issue has an owner and a date.

**Total hands-on effort:** Approximately **14–19 hours**, shared across the coordinator, owner, and bakery staff. These are work estimates, not elapsed time or vendor-cost estimates.

### Keep the Launch Small
This plan covers **one newsletter, one reusable template, one signup form, one welcome message, and basic signup promotion**. It does not require a website rebuild, paid ads, complex automation, or professional photography.

If approvals or technical setup slip, simplify the design and skip the promotional offer—not consent checks, testing, or final approval. A short, accurate newsletter sent to a small willing audience is a successful first launch.
