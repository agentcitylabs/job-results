Six-Post Grocery Savings Thread

1/6

Before shopping, check your fridge, freezer, and cupboards. Build your list around what you already have, so you buy fewer duplicates and waste less food.

2/6

Plan 3–4 flexible meals, not 7 rigid ones. Choose recipes that share ingredients, like spinach for pasta, omelets, and smoothies.

3/6

Compare prices by unit, not package size. The bigger box isn’t always cheaper, and store brands often offer the same basics for less.

4/6

Shop with a list—and eat first. Hunger makes snacks and impulse buys much harder to resist. If it isn’t on the list, leave it for next time.

5/6

Check the reduced-price section for produce, meat, and bakery items. Use or freeze them soon, and save the biggest discounts for meals you already planned.

6/6

Make waste harder: freeze ripe fruit, herbs, bread, and leftovers. A “use first” shelf in your fridge can turn forgotten food into tomorrow’s dinner.
