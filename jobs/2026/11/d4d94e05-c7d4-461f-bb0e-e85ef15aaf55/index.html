The work, as delivered

Delivered two ad script options, each laid out across 15 seconds with visuals, voiceover, on-screen text, and an audience description. The first opens with an awkward ordering moment while traveling; the second opens with a five-minute learning challenge. Notes identify assumed app features and ask the buyer to confirm the app name, supported features, final call to action, and any free or trial messaging.

  • “The Awkward Moment” targets travelers and first-time language learners.
  • “The Five-Minute Challenge” targets busy professionals, students, and casual learners short on study time.
  • Both scripts use four timed sections, from 0–3 seconds through 11–15 seconds.
  • Visual directions include lessons, practical language use, and progress or streak displays.
  • Both scripts include an [App Name] placeholder and a closing call to action.

Summary

Two 15-second ad script options for a language-learning app.

Each uses a different opening hook and includes voiceover, on-screen action, and a clear audience fit.

Replace [App Name] with the product name and adjust the final CTA to match the campaign destination.

Version 1: “The Awkward Moment” Hook

Audience: Travelers and first-time language learners who want practical phrases for real-life situations.

Length: 15 seconds

TimeVisualVoiceover / On-screen text
0–3 secA traveler points at a menu, struggling to pronounce a dish. The server looks confused.VO: “Ever ordered something… and had no idea what arrived?”
3–7 secQuick transition to the app. A short lesson teaches the phrase for ordering the dish.VO: “Learn useful phrases before your next trip with [App Name].”
7–11 secThe traveler confidently orders in the local language. The server smiles and nods.VO: “Just a few minutes a day can make every conversation easier.”
11–15 secApp screen shows a completed lesson and a simple streak animation.VO: “Start speaking with [App Name] today.”<br>On-screen: “Learn a language. Live more of the moment.”

Version 2: “The Five-Minute Challenge” Hook

Audience: Busy professionals, students, and casual learners who believe they do not have enough time to study.

Length: 15 seconds

TimeVisualVoiceover / On-screen text
0–3 secA timer appears: “Got 5 minutes?” A person waits for coffee and opens the app.VO: “What could you learn in five minutes?”
3–7 secFast cuts show a bite-sized vocabulary lesson, listening practice, and a pronunciation exercise.VO: “With [App Name], you can build real language skills one quick lesson at a time.”
7–11 secThe learner uses a new phrase in a text message, then confidently says it aloud.VO: “Practice anywhere. Keep your progress moving.”
11–15 secThe app displays a daily streak and progress bar.VO: “Your next language starts now. Try [App Name].”<br>On-screen: “Five minutes. One step closer.”

Notes

  • Assumed the app offers short, mobile-friendly lessons, vocabulary practice, listening, and pronunciation features.
  • Assumed a general consumer audience and a direct-response ad format for social media or mobile placements.
  • Both scripts are written to fit approximately 15 seconds at a natural, energetic speaking pace.
  • The client should confirm the app name, supported features, final CTA, and whether “free” or trial messaging should be included.
