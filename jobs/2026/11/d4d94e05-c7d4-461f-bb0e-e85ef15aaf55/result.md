## Summary

Two 15-second ad script options for a language-learning app.  
Each uses a different opening hook and includes voiceover, on-screen action, and a clear audience fit.  
Replace **[App Name]** with the product name and adjust the final CTA to match the campaign destination.

## Version 1: “The Awkward Moment” Hook

**Audience:** Travelers and first-time language learners who want practical phrases for real-life situations.

**Length:** 15 seconds

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 sec | A traveler points at a menu, struggling to pronounce a dish. The server looks confused. | **VO:** “Ever ordered something… and had no idea what arrived?” |
| 3–7 sec | Quick transition to the app. A short lesson teaches the phrase for ordering the dish. | **VO:** “Learn useful phrases before your next trip with **[App Name]**.” |
| 7–11 sec | The traveler confidently orders in the local language. The server smiles and nods. | **VO:** “Just a few minutes a day can make every conversation easier.” |
| 11–15 sec | App screen shows a completed lesson and a simple streak animation. | **VO:** “Start speaking with **[App Name]** today.”<br>**On-screen:** “Learn a language. Live more of the moment.” |

## Version 2: “The Five-Minute Challenge” Hook

**Audience:** Busy professionals, students, and casual learners who believe they do not have enough time to study.

**Length:** 15 seconds

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 sec | A timer appears: **“Got 5 minutes?”** A person waits for coffee and opens the app. | **VO:** “What could you learn in five minutes?” |
| 3–7 sec | Fast cuts show a bite-sized vocabulary lesson, listening practice, and a pronunciation exercise. | **VO:** “With **[App Name]**, you can build real language skills one quick lesson at a time.” |
| 7–11 sec | The learner uses a new phrase in a text message, then confidently says it aloud. | **VO:** “Practice anywhere. Keep your progress moving.” |
| 11–15 sec | The app displays a daily streak and progress bar. | **VO:** “Your next language starts now. Try **[App Name]**.”<br>**On-screen:** “Five minutes. One step closer.” |

## Notes

- Assumed the app offers short, mobile-friendly lessons, vocabulary practice, listening, and pronunciation features.
- Assumed a general consumer audience and a direct-response ad format for social media or mobile placements.
- Both scripts are written to fit approximately 15 seconds at a natural, energetic speaking pace.
- The client should confirm the app name, supported features, final CTA, and whether “free” or trial messaging should be included.
