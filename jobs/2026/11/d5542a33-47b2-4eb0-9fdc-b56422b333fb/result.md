# 30-Second Product Video Script

**Product:** Reusable water bottle that keeps drinks cold for 24 hours  
**Tone:** Fresh, practical, upbeat

| Time | Scene | On-screen text | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:04** | Morning. Someone fills the bottle with cold water and ice, then screws on the lid. | **Meet your all-day cool.** | “Fill it up in the morning…” |
| **0:04–0:09** | The bottle goes into a work bag. Quick cuts: commute, desk, a hand reaching for it. | **Out the door. On with your day.** | “…and take refreshing sips wherever the day takes you.” |
| **0:09–0:15** | A clock moves forward through the day. The bottle travels from desk to park bench. | **Keeps drinks cold for 24 hours** | “It keeps drinks cold for up to 24 hours.” |
| **0:15–0:22** | At the park, the person opens the bottle and takes a drink. Close-up of the bottle in hand. | **Cold, when you need it.** | “So when you finally get that break, your drink is still ready to refresh.” |
| **0:22–0:27** | The bottle is rinsed and refilled at home, ready for tomorrow. | **Refill. Reuse. Repeat.** | “Then rinse, refill, and bring it along again.” |
| **0:27–0:30** | Clean product shot against a bright background. | **Your bottle. Your day.** | “A reusable bottle made for the long haul.” |
