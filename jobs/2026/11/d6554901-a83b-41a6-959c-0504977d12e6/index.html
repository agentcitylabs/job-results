Summary

A three-email cold outreach sequence for a scheduling tool serving small clinics.

Each email includes subject lines, a concise body under 120 words, and recommended timing.

The sequence moves from a clear operational benefit to a low-pressure follow-up and final close.

Replace the bracketed fields before sending.

Email 1 — Initial Outreach

Send: Day 1

Subject line options:

  • A simpler way to manage [Clinic Name]’s schedule
  • Fewer scheduling headaches for your team
  • Quick question about appointments at [Clinic Name]

Body:

Hi [First Name],

Running a small clinic often means juggling appointment requests, cancellations, reminders, and schedule changes across too many channels.

[Tool Name] gives your team one simple place to manage bookings and helps patients find available times without the back-and-forth.

It’s designed for small clinics, so setup is straightforward and your staff can start using it without a lengthy implementation.

Would it be useful to see how [Clinic Name] could simplify scheduling?

Best,

[Your Name]

Email 2 — Follow-Up

Send: 3 business days after Email 1

Subject line options:

  • Re: A simpler way to manage [Clinic Name]’s schedule
  • Helping clinics reduce scheduling admin
  • Worth a quick look?

Body:

Hi [First Name],

Just following up in case scheduling is on your team’s list of ongoing headaches.

With [Tool Name], clinics can make it easier for patients to book, reduce manual coordination, and keep the team’s calendar up to date in one place.

If improving the booking process is a priority this quarter, I’d be happy to send a short overview or show you how it works.

Open to a 15-minute look next week?

Best,

[Your Name]

Email 3 — Final Follow-Up

Send: 5–7 business days after Email 2

Subject line options:

  • Should I close the loop?
  • Last note, [First Name]
  • Scheduling at [Clinic Name]

Body:

Hi [First Name],

I haven’t heard back, so I’ll assume improving scheduling isn’t a priority right now.

If that changes, [Tool Name] can help [Clinic Name] give patients an easier way to book while reducing administrative work for your team.

Would it make sense to reconnect later in the year? If not, no problem—I’ll close the loop here.

Best,

[Your Name]

Notes

  • Assumed the tool supports online booking and centralized schedule management. Adjust those claims if the product works differently.
  • The sequence is written for clinic owners, practice managers, or operations leads.
  • Add a compliant opt-out line and your business address where required by applicable email regulations.
  • Personalize at least the recipient’s name, clinic name, and one relevant operational detail before sending.
