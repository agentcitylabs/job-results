The work, as delivered

The deliverable is a 12-shot storyboard for a 45-second, 9:16 video. Each shot includes timing, framing, action and on-screen captions, moving from a finished-oats opening through preparation, refrigeration and a morning reveal. It assumes one serving of maple overnight oats topped with berries and includes ingredient quantities and editing notes.

  • Twelve timed shots spanning 0:00–0:45.
  • Overhead, side close-up and extreme close-up framing.
  • Ingredient captions with measured quantities.
  • Refrigeration caption specifying overnight, at least six hours.
  • Editing notes for caption placement, action cuts and the morning transition.
  • Matching opening and closing spoon shots to create a loop.

Overnight Oats — 45-Second Vertical Storyboard

Format: 9:16 · 45 seconds · 12 shots

Assumption: One serving of maple overnight oats, topped with berries. Use a clear, wide-mouth jar so the ingredients and texture show.

Ingredients: ½ cup rolled oats, ½ cup milk, ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt, 1 tsp chia seeds, 1 tsp maple syrup, and a handful of berries.

Edit notes

  • Keep captions high-contrast and inside the central safe area, clear of the jar and bottom interface controls.
  • Cut on pours, spoon movements, and the lid twist. Let the final scoop linger.
  • Use a clean hard cut from the fridge shot to the morning shot—the overnight caption makes the time jump explicit.
  • Capture the finished-oats opening shot after chilling; reuse that setup for the closing shot to create a natural loop.
