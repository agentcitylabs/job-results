# Overnight Oats — 45-Second Vertical Storyboard

**Format:** 9:16 · 45 seconds · 12 shots  
**Assumption:** One serving of maple overnight oats, topped with berries. Use a clear, wide-mouth jar so the ingredients and texture show.

**Ingredients:** ½ cup rolled oats, ½ cup milk, ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt, 1 tsp chia seeds, 1 tsp maple syrup, and a handful of berries.

| Shot | Time | Framing & action | On-screen caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | **Extreme close-up:** Lift a spoonful of finished oats, with a berry on top. Hold briefly so the creamy texture reads. | **Breakfast, already made.** |
| 2 | 0:03–0:05 | **Medium overhead:** Set an empty jar in the center of the counter. Ingredients sit within reach around it. | **Let’s prep tonight.** |
| 3 | 0:05–0:09 | **Tight overhead:** Pour measured rolled oats into the jar. Cut as the last flakes fall. | **½ cup rolled oats** |
| 4 | 0:09–0:13 | **Side close-up:** Pour in milk. Keep the rising liquid level visible through the glass. | **½ cup milk** |
| 5 | 0:13–0:17 | **Tight 45° angle:** Spoon in Greek yogurt; scrape the measuring cup clean. | **¼ cup plain Greek yogurt** |
| 6 | 0:17–0:20 | **Overhead close-up:** Sprinkle chia seeds across the yogurt. | **1 tsp chia seeds** |
| 7 | 0:20–0:23 | **Side close-up:** Drizzle maple syrup from a measuring spoon. | **1 tsp maple syrup** |
| 8 | 0:23–0:28 | **Tight overhead:** Stir thoroughly, reaching the bottom and scraping the sides until no dry oats remain. | **Stir well—get the bottom!** |
| 9 | 0:28–0:31 | **Medium close-up:** Screw the lid onto the jar. Finish with a quick, satisfying twist. | **Cover.** |
| 10 | 0:31–0:35 | **Fridge-level medium:** Slide the jar onto a refrigerator shelf. Hold long enough to read the chilling instruction. | **Refrigerate overnight**<br>**At least 6 hours** |
| 11 | 0:35–0:41 | **Overhead close-up, next morning:** Open the chilled jar, give the oats a quick stir, then add berries. Use a small jump cut between stirring and topping. | First: **Morning: stir.**<br>Then: **Top with berries.** |
| 12 | 0:41–0:45 | **Extreme close-up:** Scoop through the berries and oats. Match the opening angle and lift the spoon toward the lens. | **No cooking. Just breakfast.** |

### Edit notes
- Keep captions high-contrast and inside the central safe area, clear of the jar and bottom interface controls.
- Cut on pours, spoon movements, and the lid twist. Let the final scoop linger.
- Use a clean hard cut from the fridge shot to the morning shot—the overnight caption makes the time jump explicit.
- Capture the finished-oats opening shot after chilling; reuse that setup for the closing shot to create a natural loop.
