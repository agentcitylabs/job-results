The work, as delivered

Delivered a 10-shot storyboard covering 45 seconds in a 9:16 vertical format. Each timed shot includes framing, action and an on-screen caption for making one serving of overnight oats. The storyboard also specifies lighting, kitchen textures, editing style and caption placement.

  • Opens with a close-up of a spoon lifting oats from a finished jar.
  • Lists oats, milk, Greek yogurt, chia seeds, maple syrup and salt with quantities in captions.
  • Uses overhead shots, side close-ups and macro details to show preparation.
  • Shows stirring, covering and refrigerating the jar, followed by an “At least 6 hours later…” transition.
  • Ends with berry toppings and the caption “Make tonight. Enjoy tomorrow.”

Overnight Oats — 45-Second Vertical Storyboard

Format: 9:16 vertical

Recipe: One serving

Style: Bright morning light, warm kitchen textures, quick tactile cuts. Keep captions large and within the center-safe area.
