The work, as delivered

Delivered a newsletter intro titled “Three new arrivals, and a seat saved for you.” It introduces three books and invites readers to a monthly reading evening. An opening note states that the book selections and event details are illustrative, and that “new arrivals” means newly stocked rather than newly published.

  • Introduces James by Percival Everett.
  • Introduces The Serviceberry by Robin Wall Kimmerer.
  • Introduces The Thursday Murder Club by Richard Osman.
  • Proposes an illustrative reading evening on the month’s last Thursday, from 6:30 to 7:30 pm in the shop.
  • Invites guests to read a favourite passage or simply listen, with tea and biscuits.
  • Closes with an invitation to browse and ask for help choosing a book.

Assumption: The arrivals and event details below are illustrative; “new arrivals” means newly stocked, not newly published.

Three new arrivals, and a seat saved for you

There’s a particular pleasure in opening a fresh box of books: the rustle of packing paper, the first glimpse of a cover, the immediate temptation to stop working and start reading. This month, three arrivals have us reaching for bookmarks.

James by Percival Everett gives Jim from Adventures of Huckleberry Finn the centre of the story in a novel full of danger, wit and fierce intelligence.

For something quieter, The Serviceberry by Robin Wall Kimmerer starts with a small, sweet fruit and opens into generous questions about what we owe one another.

And if your ideal evening involves a comfortable chair and a thoroughly inconvenient murder, The Thursday Murder Club by Richard Osman is waiting for you.

We’d also love to see you at our Monthly Reading Evening on the last Thursday of this month, from 6:30 to 7:30 pm, here in the shop. Bring a favourite passage to read aloud, or simply come and listen. There’s no homework and no pressure to speak; we’ll provide tea and a few biscuits.

Whether you’re browsing for yourself or choosing a gift for someone tricky, pop in. We’re always happy to help you find your next book.

See you between the shelves.
