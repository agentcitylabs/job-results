# SOP: New Hire’s First Day

**Purpose:** Give every new hire a welcoming, organized first day and the tools and context they need to begin work.  
**Scope:** Applies to all new employees at the agency.  
**Assumption:** This SOP is for an in-person first day; for a remote hire, the manager sends setup details and joins meetings by video.

## Owners

- **Operations Lead:** Coordinates the day, paperwork, access, and logistics. If there is no Operations Lead, the Founder owns these tasks.
- **Hiring Manager:** Sets role expectations, introduces the hire to the team, and explains current work.
- **IT / Systems Owner:** Prepares equipment, accounts, and access. This can be the Operations Lead at a small agency.
- **New Hire:** Completes required forms, tests access, and asks questions when something is unclear.

## First-day steps

| When | Step | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Before arrival** | Confirm the start time, office location, arrival contact, and any items to bring. Prepare the desk, computer, email, calendar, chat, file storage, and project-management access. Grant only the access needed for the role. | Operations Lead; IT / Systems Owner |
| **9:00** | Welcome the new hire, show them where to work, and introduce them to the team. Share the day’s schedule and explain where to find help. | Operations Lead |
| **9:30** | Complete employment and payroll forms, confirm emergency contact details, and review how to record time off and working hours. Store personal information securely. | Operations Lead |
| **10:15** | Set up and test the computer, email, chat, shared files, calendar, design software, and password manager. Confirm the hire can access the tools they need. | IT / Systems Owner |
| **11:00** | Walk through agency basics: who does what, how projects move from brief to delivery, where files live, naming conventions, review and approval steps, and client communication norms. | Hiring Manager |
| **12:00** | Have lunch with the team or a designated onboarding buddy. Keep it informal and leave space for questions. | Hiring Manager or Onboarding Buddy |
| **1:00** | Review the new hire’s role, first-week priorities, current project context, and who will review their work. Show a relevant project brief and examples of finished work. | Hiring Manager |
| **2:00** | Give a small, low-risk first task or guided exercise. Explain the expected outcome, deadline, and where to ask for feedback. Avoid assigning client-critical work before access and context are confirmed. | Hiring Manager |
| **3:00** | Check in on tool access and answer questions. Fix any access issues or note who will resolve them and by when. | Operations Lead; IT / Systems Owner |
| **4:30** | Recap what the hire learned, confirm the next day’s plan, and ask what would make onboarding clearer. Share the first-week schedule and key contacts. | Hiring Manager |
| **5:00** | Confirm the hire knows how to secure equipment and sign out of shared systems. Close out any outstanding setup tasks. | Operations Lead; New Hire |

## First-day checklist

### Before the new hire arrives
- [ ] Start time, location, and arrival contact sent
- [ ] Desk and equipment ready
- [ ] Email, calendar, chat, and required software set up
- [ ] File and project access prepared with appropriate permissions
- [ ] First-day schedule and onboarding buddy confirmed

### During the day
- [ ] Welcome and team introductions completed
- [ ] Employment and payroll paperwork completed or follow-up owner and deadline recorded
- [ ] Core tools tested successfully
- [ ] Agency workflow and file conventions explained
- [ ] Role, first-week priorities, and review process discussed
- [ ] First task or guided exercise assigned with clear expectations
- [ ] Questions and access issues captured with an owner

### Before the new hire leaves
- [ ] First-week schedule shared
- [ ] Next day’s start time and plan confirmed
- [ ] Equipment and account security covered
- [ ] Any unresolved items assigned to a named owner with a due date
- [ ] Manager check-in completed

**Completion standard:** The first day is complete when the new hire knows who to contact, can access the essential tools, understands their immediate priorities, and has a clear plan for the next day.
