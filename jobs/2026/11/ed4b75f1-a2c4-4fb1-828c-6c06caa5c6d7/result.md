# Coffee Shop Customer Satisfaction Survey

**Intro for customers:**  
Thanks for visiting us. This survey takes about two minutes. Your feedback helps us improve the coffee, service, and overall experience.

## Survey Questions

| # | Question | Answer type |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Overall, how satisfied were you with your visit today?** | **5-point rating scale:** Very dissatisfied / Dissatisfied / Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied / Satisfied / Very satisfied |
| 2 | **How would you rate the quality of your drink?** | **5-point rating scale:** Very poor / Poor / Fair / Good / Excellent |
| 3 | **How would you rate the quality and freshness of the food or pastries you ordered?** | **5-point rating scale**, plus **“Did not order food”** option: Very poor / Poor / Fair / Good / Excellent |
| 4 | **How satisfied were you with the speed of service?** | **5-point rating scale:** Very dissatisfied / Dissatisfied / Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied / Satisfied / Very satisfied |
| 5 | **How friendly and helpful was our staff?** | **5-point rating scale:** Not at all friendly / Slightly friendly / Moderately friendly / Very friendly / Extremely friendly |
| 6 | **How would you rate the cleanliness and atmosphere of the shop?** | **5-point rating scale:** Very poor / Poor / Fair / Good / Excellent |
| 7 | **How likely are you to visit us again within the next month?** | **5-point likelihood scale:** Very unlikely / Unlikely / Not sure / Likely / Very likely |
| 8 | **What is one thing we could improve, and what did you enjoy most about your visit?** | **Open-text response** |

## How to Read the Results

### 1. Track the average and positive-score percentage

For Questions 1–7, assign scores from **1 to 5**:

- 1 = most negative response  
- 5 = most positive response  

For each question, review:

- **Average score:** The overall strength of the result.  
- **Positive-score percentage:** The percentage selecting 4 or 5.  
- **Negative-score percentage:** The percentage selecting 1 or 2.  

As a simple guide:

- **4.3–5.0:** Excellent  
- **3.8–4.2:** Good, with some room to improve  
- **3.0–3.7:** Mixed experience; investigate further  
- **Below 3.0:** Priority issue  

Report results only when there are enough responses to protect privacy and avoid overreacting to a very small sample.

### 2. Identify the strongest and weakest parts of the experience

Compare the average scores for drink quality, food, service speed, staff, and atmosphere.

- The **highest-scoring area** is a strength to protect and promote.
- The **lowest-scoring area** is the clearest improvement opportunity.
- A low score with many negative responses deserves more urgent attention than a slightly lower average caused by mostly neutral answers.

### 3. Review repeat-visit intent separately

Question 7 is a useful indicator of customer loyalty. Compare it with overall satisfaction:

- **High satisfaction and high likelihood to return:** The shop is creating a strong customer experience.
- **High satisfaction but low return intent:** Check pricing, convenience, location, product variety, or visit frequency.
- **Low satisfaction but high return intent:** Customers may value the shop despite specific problems; use comments to find what needs fixing.

### 4. Look for patterns in written comments

Group responses to Question 8 into themes such as:

- Drink taste or consistency
- Waiting time
- Staff friendliness
- Cleanliness or seating
- Prices or value
- Food and pastry selection
- Noise, Wi-Fi, or atmosphere
- Positive experiences

Count how often each theme appears and read several examples. Prioritize themes that are both **frequent** and **linked to low satisfaction scores**.

### 5. Turn findings into actions

After each survey period, choose:

1. **One issue to fix immediately**
2. **One strength to maintain**
3. **One change to test**

Repeat the survey regularly—such as monthly or quarterly—to see whether scores improve after changes are made.
