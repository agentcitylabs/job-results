The work, as delivered

The delivery contains three 30-second scripts titled “All-Day Commute,” “Move, Pause, Refresh” and “One Bottle, Many Plans.” Each includes two alternative opening hooks and timed tables covering scenes, on-screen text and voice-over. Notes explain how to use the hooks and ask for confirmation of whether “up to 24 hours” is the preferred claim wording before filming or publishing.

  • Commute, workout and changing-plans scenarios
  • Two interchangeable 0–3 second hooks per script

  • Visual directions, on-screen text and voice-over for every timed segment
  • Product details limited to reusability and the 24-hour cold claim

Summary

Three 30-second scripts for a reusable water bottle that keeps drinks cold for 24 hours.

Each script includes two opening-hook options; choose one, then use the shared scenes that follow.

Timings, visuals, on-screen text and voice-over are ready to take into production.
