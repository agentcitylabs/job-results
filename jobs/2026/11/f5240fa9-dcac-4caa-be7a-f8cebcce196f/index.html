Four-Week Plan: Launch a Local Bakery Newsletter

Assumption: The bakery is starting from scratch. The goal is to send its first newsletter by the end of Week 4, then continue monthly. Time estimates are hands-on work, not waiting time.

Who could do it

Up to four people could cover the project; one person can take multiple roles:

  1. Bakery owner: Approves the offer, budget, and final email.
  2. Newsletter lead: A staff member who coordinates the work, collects content, and manages signups.
  3. Copywriter: Writes the signup invitation and newsletter.
  4. Email specialist: Sets up the email tool, designs the template, and handles testing and sending.

Key Dependencies

  • Set up the platform and test the signup form before promoting signups.
  • Confirm the featured product and offer before writing the email.
  • Get owner approval and complete testing before sending.

What “Launched” Means

By the end of Week 4, the bakery has:

  • A working signup route and a permission-based subscriber list.
  • A reusable, mobile-friendly newsletter template.
  • Its first newsletter sent.
  • A named owner and a date for the next issue.

Keep the first launch small: one featured product, one clear action, and one good photo. A useful email sent on time beats a complicated campaign that never leaves the draft folder.
