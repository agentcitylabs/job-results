The work, as delivered

Delivered a 30-second product video script in a table with six timed scenes, on-screen text and voice-over. The sequence moves from a warm-drink opening to the bottle reveal, daily use, a next-day sip and a closing product shot. An audio note calls for bright, easygoing music and ice-and-pour sounds.

  • Six scenes spanning 0:00–0:30
  • Opening text: “Still warm by lunchtime?”
  • Bottle reveal with ice, water and the 24-hour cold claim
  • Desk, bag and outdoor scenes followed by a next-day time jump
  • Closing bottle and brand logo shot
  • Final text: “Keep it cold. Keep it going.”
