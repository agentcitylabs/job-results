# 30-Second Product Video Script

| Time | Scene | On-screen text | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:04** | Close-up: someone takes a sip from a bottle on a sunny afternoon, then makes a “not cold anymore” face. | **Still warm by lunchtime?** | “Your drink shouldn’t give up before you do.” |
| **0:04–0:08** | Reveal the reusable bottle. Fill it with ice and water; snap on the lid. | **Meet your everyday bottle.** | “Meet the reusable bottle made to keep drinks cold for 24 hours.” |
| **0:08–0:13** | The bottle goes along through a busy day: desk, bag, quick stop outdoors. | **Cold for 24 hours.** | “Fill it in the morning…” |
| **0:13–0:18** | Evening-to-morning time jump. Hand reaches for the bottle the next day. | **Morning to tomorrow.** | “…and come back to a cold sip.” |
| **0:18–0:24** | Pour a drink over ice, then take a refreshing sip. | **Refill. Sip. Repeat.** | “One bottle, ready to go wherever your day takes you.” |
| **0:24–0:30** | Clean hero shot of the bottle beside a glass. End on the bottle and brand logo. | **Keep it cold. Keep it going.** | “Make the switch to reusable. Your next cold sip starts here.” |

**Audio:** Bright, easygoing music throughout; crisp ice-and-pour sounds on the reveal.
